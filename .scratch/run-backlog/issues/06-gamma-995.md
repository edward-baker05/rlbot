# 06 - `gaeGamma` 0.99 -> 0.995

Status: open
Blocked by: p18entropy

## Why anything wants it

Every consequence-keyed term pays late, and 0.99 discounts hard:

| signal | steps out | gamma 0.99 | gamma 0.995 |
|---|---|---|---|
| opponent saves your shot | ~42 | 0.66 | 0.81 |
| next touch (possession, item 01) | ~83 | 0.43 | 0.66 |
| 4-5 save rebound sequence (item 10) | 150-225 | 0.105 | 0.325 |

The last row is the important one: at 0.99 the eventual payoff of sustained
pressure is discounted ~6x more than the save penalty that lands against it.

## Why it was frozen in p17

Two reasons, both still valid. It is a second variable; and p17 seeded from a
2.28B-step checkpoint whose critic was fit under 0.99, so changing gamma makes
every inherited value estimate wrong everywhere at once -- a far larger
perturbation than the term under test.

Take it on a run that either starts fresh or explicitly budgets for critic
recovery, and expect `Critic/V All` and `GAE/Avg Val Target` to move a lot
before anything else means anything.
